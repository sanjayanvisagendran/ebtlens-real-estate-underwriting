# Methodology

## Operating forecast

Gross potential income equals gross leasable area multiplied by base rent and other income per square foot. Vacancy loss is deducted from gross potential income. Operating expenses are calculated per square foot. Net operating income equals effective gross income less operating expenses.

## Valuation

The income approach divides Year 1 NOI by the underwriting capitalization rate. The comparable-sales approach averages adjusted price per square foot across five fictional transactions and multiplies the average by the subject property's area. Debt sizing uses the lower valuation.

## Loan sizing

The model calculates four capacities:

1. Requested loan amount.
2. Underwritten value multiplied by maximum LTV.
3. NOI divided by minimum DSCR and the annual mortgage constant.
4. NOI divided by minimum debt yield.

The recommended loan is the lowest capacity rounded down to the specified loan increment.

## Amortization

Monthly principal and interest payments use a fixed interest rate and a 25-year amortization period. The five-year schedule produces a balloon balance at maturity.

## Sensitivity analysis

The Excel workbook evaluates DSCR across vacancy and interest-rate combinations. It also evaluates LTV across capitalization rates and valuation adjustments. These are deterministic scenarios, not probability-weighted forecasts.
