# Harbour Logistics Centre

## Preliminary debt investment memorandum

**Status:** Proceed with conditions  
**Recommended loan:** CAD 33.5 million  
**Property:** Fictional 200,000-square-foot industrial logistics facility in Metro Vancouver  
**Purpose:** Portfolio learning case; not an actual financing opportunity

## Transaction summary

The fictional borrower requests a CAD 36.0 million first-mortgage loan. DebtLens recommends CAD 33.5 million after applying the lower of the requested amount, 65% maximum loan-to-value capacity, 1.30x minimum debt-service-coverage capacity and 8.0% minimum debt-yield capacity. The DSCR limit is binding.

The model estimates Year 1 net operating income of CAD 3.288 million. It values the property at CAD 57.18 million using a 5.75% capitalization rate and at CAD 56.92 million using five fictional adjusted comparable sales. The lower CAD 56.92 million result is used as the underwritten value.

## Credit strengths

- Base leverage remains below the stated maximum LTV.
- Debt yield exceeds the minimum credit requirement.
- The two valuation approaches produce similar results under the fictional assumptions.
- Rent growth exceeds operating-expense growth in the five-year base forecast.

## Principal risks

1. **Limited DSCR headroom.** The recommended loan produces approximately 1.30x DSCR, so modest vacancy or rate deterioration can breach the minimum.
2. **Refinancing exposure.** The model projects a material balloon balance at maturity. A higher exit rate or lower NOI could restrict refinancing proceeds.
3. **Tenant and lease risk.** No tenant roster, lease-expiry schedule, credit assessment or rent-roll verification is included.
4. **Physical and environmental risk.** No property-condition assessment, environmental report or capital plan is included.
5. **Borrower risk.** Sponsor liquidity, net worth, experience and guarantees are not modelled.
6. **Market evidence.** All comparable transactions are synthetic and cannot support a real valuation conclusion.

## Conditions before approval

- Validate the rent roll, leases, recoveries, arrears and tenant concentrations.
- Review borrower financial statements, ownership structure and contingent liabilities.
- Obtain independent appraisal, environmental and building-condition reports.
- Confirm title, insurance, zoning and permitted use.
- Review capital requirements and establish reserves where appropriate.
- Re-run the downside case using verified third-party and borrower data.

## Recommendation

Proceed only to further due diligence at a maximum initial loan amount of CAD 33.5 million. The recommendation is conditional because DSCR is the binding limit and the case omits material borrower, lease, legal and physical-property evidence.
