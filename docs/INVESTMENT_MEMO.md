# Harbour Logistics Centre

## Preliminary debt investment memorandum
**Status:** Proceed with conditions  
**Recommended loan:** CAD 33.5 million  
**Property:** Fictional 200,000-square-foot industrial logistics facility in Metro Vancouver  
**Purpose:** Portfolio learning case; not an actual financing opportunity

## Transaction summary
The fictional borrower requests a CAD 36.0 million first-mortgage loan. DebtLens recommends CAD 33.5 million after applying a 65% maximum LTV, 1.30x minimum DSCR and 8.0% minimum debt yield. DSCR is binding. The lower of the income value and five fictional comparable sales is used for sizing.

The maturity review capitalizes Year 5 NOI at 6.00% and tests the CAD 30.02 million balloon at a 6.75% refinance rate. Under these assumptions, maturity LTV is 48.4% and refinance DSCR is 1.50x. These outputs are model scenarios, not evidence that refinancing is available.

## Credit strengths
- Base leverage remains below the stated maximum LTV.
- Debt yield exceeds the minimum credit requirement.
- The two valuation approaches produce similar results under fictional assumptions.
- Base maturity LTV and refinance DSCR pass the stated scenario tests.

## Principal risks
1. **Limited initial DSCR headroom.** The recommendation is sized almost exactly to 1.30x.
2. **Refinancing exposure.** The balloon depends on future NOI, value, rates and lender availability.
3. **Tenant and lease risk.** No tenant roster, expiries, credit assessment or rent-roll verification is included.
4. **Physical and environmental risk.** No condition assessment, environmental report or capital plan is included.
5. **Borrower risk.** Sponsor liquidity, net worth, experience and guarantees are not modelled.
6. **Market evidence.** All comparables are synthetic and cannot support an actual valuation.

## Conditions before approval
- Validate rent roll, leases, recoveries, arrears and tenant concentrations.
- Review borrower financial statements, ownership structure and contingent liabilities.
- Obtain independent appraisal, environmental and building-condition reports.
- Confirm title, insurance, zoning and permitted use.
- Review capital requirements and establish reserves where appropriate.
- Re-run base and refinance cases using verified third-party and borrower data.

## Recommendation
Proceed only to further due diligence at a maximum initial loan amount of CAD 33.5 million. The recommendation remains conditional because initial DSCR is binding and material borrower, lease, legal and physical-property evidence is absent.
