# Review questions

Use these questions to prepare for a technical or investment interview.

1. Why does the model use the lower of the income and comparable valuations?
2. Why is DSCR the binding constraint in the base case?
3. How does a higher interest rate reduce DSCR capacity without changing property value?
4. How would an interest-only period affect coverage and maturity risk?
5. Which lease and borrower documents are required before relying on the NOI?
6. Why can debt yield be useful when amortization and interest rates differ between loans?
7. What weaknesses arise from using adjusted price per square foot as the comparable metric?
8. How would construction financing differ from this stabilized-property model?
9. Why does a five-year loan with 25-year amortization create refinancing risk?
10. Which assumptions should be supported by external evidence in a real underwriting process?
